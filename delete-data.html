Delete your Zone Rush data

App: Zone Rush (com.gemmyapps.zonerush) · Developer: GEM CREATORS · Contact: gemmyapps@gmail.com

Zone Rush has no accounts of its own: there is no username, password or email sign-up. Your game data lives only on your device, and you can delete it yourself at any time. If you use optional online rooms, your IP address also reaches the services and players listed below.

Option 1: delete in the game (recommended)

  1. Open Zone Rush and tap Settings on the main menu.
  2. Tap Delete my data.
  3. Read the confirmation and tap Delete everything.
What this does: erases all progress, XP, gems, cosmetics, stats and the error log on this device. It cannot be undone.

Good to know: the season pass and supporter pack you paid for come back with Shop → Restore purchases, because Google Play keeps that record.

Option 2: delete without opening the game

  • Local data: Android Settings → Apps → Zone Rush → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.

Option 3: ask us

Email gemmyapps@gmail.com with the subject “Zone Rush data deletion”. Because we have no account system, we hold no personal data on our own servers. We will delete any email correspondence within 30 days and help you with the steps above. We will never ask for your password.

Data we keep

We keep nothing after deletion. Google keeps purchase records under its own policies, and the services used for online rooms are outside our control.
