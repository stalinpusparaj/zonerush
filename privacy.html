Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026 · Developer: GEM CREATORS · Contact: gemmyapps@gmail.com

1. Summary

Zone Rush is a single-player game. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. We do not run our own game servers and we do not sell personal data.

The Android app uses one Google service, Google Play Billing, described in section 3. The app shows no ads, has no analytics and no crash reporting, and does not use Google Play Games sign-in.

2. Data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, cosmetics, gem balance and stats are stored locally on your device (app/WebView storage). You can erase them at any time with Settings → Delete my data in the game, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Third-party services (Android app only)

ServiceWhat it processesWhyOptional?
Google Play BillingPurchase records, handled by Google Play. We receive only an order id and the item bought, never your payment details.Processing optional cosmetic purchases and restoring themYes. Purchases are cosmetic only and never required.

Google's policies apply to these services: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. What we do not collect

No precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, videos, audio, messages, browsing history, health data or files. There is no in-game chat, no multiplayer and no user-to-user communication.

5. Security

Purchase data sent by Google Play Billing leaves your device encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

6. Your choices and data deletion

  • In the game: Settings → Delete my data erases local progress, gems, cosmetics and stats.
  • Purchases: your Google Play order history is kept by Google; see it or request deletion in your Google Account.
  • We hold no other copy of your data. Questions: gemmyapps@gmail.com.

Full steps: Delete your Zone Rush data.

7. Children

Zone Rush is not directed to children under 13. Its target audience is 13 and older, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data, contact gemmyapps@gmail.com and we will delete it.

8. Changes

We will update this page when the app changes and change the “Last updated” date above.
